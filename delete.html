Delete your Zivo account

How to remove your account and all data tied to it.

Zivo is a focus timer made by an independent developer (Bilel, Tunisia). You can delete your Zivo account and everything tied to it, from inside the app or by email. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Zivo.
  2. Open Settings and tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, under Log out.
  4. Press and hold Delete for good.

Your cloud save and your Zivo login are deleted right away, and the phone starts again as a new guest.

Google may ask you to sign in once more first, to make sure it is you.

By email

If you cannot open the app, write to lightaura625@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account, with the subject “Delete my Zivo account”. To be sure the request is really from you, we reply asking you to confirm, and delete only after you answer “YES”. Your account and your save are deleted within 30 days.

We cannot reach your phone. Remove the app, or your phone may keep its own copy of your progress.

What is deleted

  • Your Zivo login: the email address, name, picture link and ids that Firebase Authentication keeps for Zivo.
  • Your cloud save: your focus minutes and level, gems, streaks, focused days, buddies, badges and the name on your player card.
  • On your phone, when you delete in the app: your progress, your account details and the picture you picked.

What is kept

Nothing on our side. Zivo keeps no copy of a deleted account. Google says it clears its own backup copies within 180 days.

If Zivo is also on another phone with the same account, remove the app there too. That phone may still hold its own copy of your progress.

What is not touched

Your Google account itself is not touched.

Zivo may still be listed in your Google Account under third-party apps. You can remove it there.

There is nothing to cancel first: Zivo has no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

If you never signed in

A guest has no account, and nothing is kept outside the phone. To remove a guest’s data, remove the app.

If you once started to sign in and it did not finish, a Zivo login with your email address may already exist. To remove it, sign in again and delete the account, or write to us.
