Zivo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Zivo is a focus timer that rewards you while you focus. It is made by an independent developer (Bilel, Tunisia). This policy explains what the app keeps, where it is kept, and how you stay in control.

Questions? Write to lightaura625@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can use Zivo without an account, as a guest. A guest’s progress stays on the phone and is sent nowhere.
  • Signing in with Google is your choice. It backs up your progress, so it comes back on a new phone.
  • No ads. No tracking. Your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and your save at any time, inside the app.

If you play as a guest

Everything Zivo knows about you stays on your phone: your focus minutes and level, your gems, your streak, the days you focused, your buddies, your badges and your settings.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Android’s own phone backup is switched off for Zivo. So a guest’s progress is not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone. If you remove the app, it is gone.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you tap “Continue with Google”, Google shows its own sign-in box. If you say yes, Zivo gets these things: your name, your email address, the link to your Google profile picture and the id number of your Google account. Firebase then makes a new id for your Zivo login.

Your Zivo login is made as soon as Google says yes, even if the first backup then fails.

Here is where each one is kept:

  • Your Zivo login. Google’s Firebase Authentication service keeps your email address, your name, the picture link, both ids, and the times you first and last signed in. This is how Zivo knows it is you next time.
  • Your phone. The app keeps a small copy of your first name, your email address, your Zivo login id, the picture link and the picture itself, so it can show them at once, even offline.
  • Your cloud save. It holds game data only (the full list is below). Your email address, your Google name and your picture are not in it.

Zivo never sees your Google password.

What is in your cloud save

When you are signed in, a copy of your progress is kept in one small save that belongs to your account. It holds exactly this:

  • The gems you hold.
  • Your focus minutes. Your level is worked out from them.
  • Your streak now, and your best streak.
  • The days you focused. Dates only, no times of day.
  • How many minutes you focused on each day, for the last 400 days.
  • How many sessions you finished and how many surprises you caught.
  • The buddies you own, and the one you picked.
  • The focus screens you own, and the one you picked.
  • The names of the badges you earned.
  • How many Streak Freezes and Double Gems you got in the Gem Shop, and how many you used.
  • How many daily gifts you took, and the day of the last one. A date only, no time of day.
  • How many gems you spent to relight a lost streak, in total.
  • The name on your player card, only if you typed one.
  • A few technical things: a format number, a random one-time code that names neither you nor your phone, and the times the save was made and last changed.

The picture you pick for your player card stays on your phone. It is never uploaded. To pick it, Zivo opens your phone’s own picture chooser and gets only the one picture you choose.

Your settings (theme, sound, session length and so on) stay on your phone too.

What Zivo does not do

  • No ads, and no ad networks.
  • No analytics and no tracking tools.
  • Zivo does not use your location, your contacts, your microphone or your camera.
  • Your data is never sold, and never shared for marketing.

Why Zivo uses your data

  • To back up your progress and bring it back when you sign in on another phone.
  • To show your name and picture on your player card.
  • To keep saves honest: the server refuses a save with impossible numbers.
  • To check that requests come from the real Zivo app (the security check below).

It is used for nothing else.

Our legal grounds (for people in the UK, EU and EEA): we use your data to do what you ask, such as backing up your save and showing your player card. We also have a legitimate interest in keeping Zivo working and keeping saves honest and secure. Signing in is your choice: you can use Zivo as a guest, with no account and no cloud data.

Who keeps your data

Zivo runs on Google’s Firebase services. Google stores the data for Zivo and follows Zivo’s instructions. No other company gets it.

  • Your login is kept by Firebase Authentication, which Google runs from data centres in the United States.
  • Your cloud save is kept in Cloud Firestore, in Google’s data centres in Europe (region eur3).

When the app talks to Google’s servers, they see your phone’s internet address (IP address) and basic technical details, like every internet service does. Google says it keeps sign-in IP addresses for a few weeks, for security.

This means some of your data is handled outside your own country — for example your login on Google’s servers in the United States. Google protects data moved between countries using the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Google’s own sign-in box follows Google’s privacy policy.

The security check (App Check)

Whenever Zivo talks to Firebase (for example to sign you in, back up your progress or delete your account), it sends along a pass from Google. The pass shows that the request comes from the real, unchanged Zivo app (the version Google Play gives out). This uses Google’s Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity. Once Firebase starts to require the pass, it helps keep changed copies of the app and scripts out of your cloud save.

For the check, your phone sends Google Play:

  • The app’s name, version and signing certificate.
  • Whether the Google accounts on the phone got the app from Google Play (its licence status).
  • Device integrity information made by Google Play services, including a key attestation certificate and a device attestation token.

Firebase then gets the result, with the same basic technical details as every request. For example: the Android version, the phone model and brand, the store the app came from, the app’s ids and the days the app was recently used.

  • It never includes your progress, your name or your email address.
  • It runs when Zivo talks to Firebase and the last pass has run out (a pass lasts about an hour). While you are signed in and Zivo is open, Firebase also renews your sign-in by itself about once an hour, and the check can run then too. If you only play as a guest, it never runs.
  • It is a security check, not tracking. Google’s rules for Play Integrity forbid using it to track people or phones.
  • Firebase App Check keeps none of the check data, and each pass expires after about an hour. Google Play handles its part under the Google Play Terms of Service: the data is encrypted, not given to anyone else, and deleted after a fixed time.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until you log out, delete your account, or remove the app.
  • In the cloud: until you delete your account.

Logging out takes your account details off the phone. Your progress is taken off too, once the cloud has confirmed it holds your latest save. Your phone settings (theme, sound) stay.

After a delete, Google says it clears its own backup copies within 180 days.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time. It cannot be undone.

  • In the app: open Settings, tap Account, tap “Delete account”, then press and hold “Delete for good”. Your cloud save and your Zivo login are deleted right away, and the phone starts again as a new guest.
  • By email: write to lightaura625@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account. To be sure the request is really from you, we reply asking you to confirm, and delete only after you answer “YES”. Your account and your save are deleted within 30 days.

Your Google account itself is not touched.

If Zivo is also on another phone with the same account, remove the app there too. That phone may still hold its own copy of your progress.

The same steps are on the web: saidanin.github.io/zivo-legal/delete.html

A guest has no account, and nothing is kept outside the phone. To remove a guest’s data, remove the app.

If you once started to sign in and it did not finish, a Zivo login with your email address may already exist. To remove it, sign in again and delete the account, or write to us.

How your data is protected

  • Data travels between the app and Google’s servers encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Server rules let a signed-in person read and change only their own save. Nobody can list the players or open another player’s save.
  • The developer can open the database, only to fix a problem or to carry out a delete request.

Children

Zivo is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has signed in, write to us and we will delete the account.

In some countries the age is higher (up to 16 in parts of Europe). There, please ask a parent before you sign in.

Your rights

You can ask what data Zivo holds about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to lightaura625@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

If you are in the UK, EU or EEA, you also have the right to complain to your country’s data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top changes with it. Important changes will also be shown in the app’s store listing.
